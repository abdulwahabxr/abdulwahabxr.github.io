About

I'm a game developer who spends equal time thinking about frame budgets and player feel.

Over the last 7 years I've shipped Unity mobile games end to end — gameplay systems, C# architecture, and the relentless optimization that keeps them running smoothly on mid-range devices. For the past 2 years I've focused on AR, most recently building an HVAC training & simulation app that lets technicians practice on virtual equipment before touching the real thing.

I care about the details that make interactive experiences feel solid: responsive input, honest performance, and interfaces that get out of the player's way.
